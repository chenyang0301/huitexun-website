🎧 Enjoy Edition · Quick Start

Xunmai AI Tour Guide (Enjoy Edition)
Quick Start Guide

Get started in 3 steps | 🌐 查看中文版
01
Device Keys
The Enjoy Edition has only four physical buttons. A long press or a single tap completes every operation. Get to know each key before you begin.
🔘
Power Key
Press & hold 3s: power on/off · Single tap: pause / resume narration
⚙️
Function Key
Press & hold 2s: turn translation / narration on or off · Single tap: volume +
🤖
AI Key
Press & hold: ask AI a question after the beep · Single tap: volume −
📡
PTT Key
Press & hold: speak after the beep · Release: listen to others
💡 Scan the QR code with WeChat to add the "Xunmai AI Tour" mini-program, then scan the device QR code to pair with the unit.
02
Key Features
🎙️
Auto Narration
  • 1 Single tap the "PTT Key" → instantly narrate nearby attractions at your current location
  • 2 Single tap the "Power Key" to pause / resume narration
🤖
AI Q&A
  • 1 Press & hold the "AI Key", ask your question after the beep
  • 2 Release the "AI Key" and wait for the spoken answer
  • 3 Single tap the "Power Key" to stop the current spoken answer
🎧
Simultaneous Interpretation
  • 1 Open the "Xunmai AI Tour" mini-program, go to "Group", and set the device to simultaneous translation mode
  • 2 Press & hold the "Function Key" for 2s to start / stop simultaneous interpretation
🌐
Translation Mode
  • 1 Press & hold the "Function Key" for 2s to enable voice translation
  • 2 Press & hold the "Function Key" for 2s again to disable translation
🎧
Bluetooth Earphone
  • 1 Turn on phone Bluetooth, open the device detail page in the "Xunmai AI Tour" mini-program, tap "Bluetooth Pairing"
  • 2 Select this unit from the phone's Bluetooth list to connect, then listen to narration via the earphone
👥
WeChat Grouping
  • 1 Scan the device QR code with WeChat to add the "Xunmai AI Tour" mini-program
  • 2 Go to the "Group" page and invite members to join the same group to team up
📡
Team Walkie-Talkie
  • 1 Press & hold the "PTT Key", speak after the beep — group members can talk at the same time
  • 2 Release the "PTT Key" to listen to other members
03
Mini-Program
Enhance Your Experience
  • ① Scan the device QR code with the WeChat mini-program to bind the device, then tap the device to enter its detail page
  • ② Turn on "Phone-Assisted Positioning" for the device to improve location accuracy
  • ③ Tap "Narration Voice" to choose your preferred narration voice
⚠️ The bound device and phone must be carried by the same person, and do not exit WeChat during use.

💡 Bluetooth Earphone Listening

After connecting Bluetooth earphones, narration and AI answers play through the earphones — private and clear, ideal for group tours.

👥 Group Walkie-Talkie

Members in the same group press the PTT key for real-time talk and release to listen — zero-distance communication between guide and visitors.
